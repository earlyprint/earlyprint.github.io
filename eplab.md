<p class="f5 lh-copy"><em>The EarlyPrint Lab offers a range of tools for the computational exploration and analysis of English print culture before 1700. We intend the Lab as a provocation, not a finished toolkit. The tools and visualizations collected here offer perspectives on the corpus that invite users to probe early English discursive history in ways that complement the search capabilities of EEBO-TCP and the Oxford English Dictionary.
</em></p>

<p class="mt4 pt3 bt b--gray f6 gray"><em>The EarlyPrint Lab</em> aims to render EEBO-TCP more tractable for quantitative historical analyses by virtue of some intensive reprocessing of the TCP texts and their metadata. The <em>Lab</em> team is led by Anupam Basu and Joseph Loewenstein, with Douglas Knox, John Ladd, and Stephen Pentecost. The <em>EarlyPrint Lab</em> site is supported by the Humanities Digital Workshop at Washington University in St. Louis.</p>
